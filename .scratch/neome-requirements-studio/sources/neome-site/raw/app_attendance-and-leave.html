Headcount is not the question. Who is here this morning is.

Three gaps account for most of what a workforce record gets wrong, and none of them is a discipline problem.

Reconstructed

Attendance is compiled at month end

A register is typed up from memory and notes, so the record is an approximation nobody can dispute or defend.

Untracked

Nobody knows what leave is left

Balances live in a spreadsheet one person maintains, so employees ask and managers guess.

Uncovered

Three people off the same day

Leave is approved one request at a time with no view of the team, and the gap is discovered on the morning.

Neome closes all three by capturing the day as it happens, showing the balance, and putting cover in front of the approver.

Attendance

Marked where the day actually starts

Check-in and check-out are recorded per employee as they happen rather than compiled afterwards, so daily attendance across a team is a fact rather than a reconstruction. Late arrivals and absences surface the same morning, which is the only time anything can be done about them.

  • Check-in and check-out
  • Daily team visibility
  • Late and absent detected

Leave

A balance the employee can see before they ask

Leave types carry their own quotas, and the balance is on the request rather than in somebody's spreadsheet. Requests route to the line manager from the directory's reporting structure, so nobody has to work out who should approve what.

  • Configurable leave types
  • Balances visible to the employee
  • Routed to the manager

Cover

Approve leave with the team calendar in view

An approver sees who else in the team is already away for those dates, so a clash is caught at the point of decision rather than on the morning it bites. Approving leave without that view is how three people end up off on the same day.

  • Team calendar on the request
  • Clash visible before approval
  • Department-wise view

Availability

Who is actually here today

Attendance and leave together answer the question a supervisor actually has, which is not how many people are employed but how many are available this morning. Combining them is what turns two records into one useful number.

  • Present today by team
  • Leave and attendance combined
  • Availability at a glance
