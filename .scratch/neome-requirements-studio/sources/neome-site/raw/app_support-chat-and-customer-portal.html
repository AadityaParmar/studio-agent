Support is slow for one reason: nobody has the context.

Three gaps account for most of the time a support desk loses, and none of them is an effort problem.

Repeated

The customer explains it again

A new person picks up the thread with none of the history, so the customer tells the story a third time.

Unrecorded

The chat ended, the problem did not

A promise made in a conversation leaves no ticket behind, so nothing chases it and nobody owns it.

Interrupting

Every status question is a phone call

Customers cannot see their own tickets or orders, so the only way to find out is to interrupt someone.

Neome closes all three by attaching the history, turning conversations into tickets, and letting customers look for themselves.

Live chat

Answer with the whole history already on screen

Chat sessions are linked to the customer, so every earlier conversation is attached before a word is typed. Agents see a queue with who is waiting and for how long, sessions are assigned rather than grabbed, and a manager can watch the room without stepping into it.

  • Customer-linked sessions
  • Full conversation history
  • Assignment and monitoring

Templates

Stop retyping the answer to the same question

Saved replies cover the questions that repeat, and they fill in the detail from the conversation rather than arriving as generic boilerplate. That keeps first-response times short and leaves agents with time for the questions that actually need thinking about.

  • Predefined responses
  • Context filled automatically
  • Faster first reply

Tickets

A conversation becomes something that can be chased

When a chat needs work beyond the conversation, it becomes a ticket automatically, carrying the customer, the conversation and any order reference with it. The chat stays linked, so whoever picks the ticket up reads what was said instead of asking the customer to repeat it.

  • Raised from the chat
  • Conversation stays attached
  • Owner and status

Customer portal

Let customers answer their own status questions

A portal gives your customer their own tickets, their order history and their past conversations, with role-based access deciding what each contact sees. Most status questions never become a call, and the ones that do start from the same record you are looking at.

  • Their tickets and orders
  • Role-based access
  • Start a chat from the portal

Time to first reply across chat and tickets, which is the number customers actually judge support on.

Who is asking most

Where is the load coming from?

Volume by customer and by topic, which usually points at a product problem or a gap in the portal rather than at the support team.
