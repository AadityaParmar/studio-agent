The workspace

One screen, no app switching

Leads, deals, activities, tasks, team chat and dashboards are one application, not a folder of separate tools. Assign a lead and its owner hears about it where they already work; close one and the pipeline total updates itself. Nothing is exported, emailed or re-keyed to move a deal along.

Deals rarely die loudly. They just go quiet.

Three gaps account for most of what a growing business loses, and none of them is a selling problem.

Unowned

Nobody's name is on the lead

Everyone assumes someone else has it, and nobody called.

Unrecorded

The deal lives in someone's head

The price was discussed. None of it was written down.

Unforecastable

You can name deals, not the number

What the pipeline is worth is a guess, not a figure.

Neome CRM closes all three by making one record carry the lead all the way to the revenue.

Capture

Every lead lands in one place, whoever sends it

One form, six labelled sections, and the company and contact carry straight through to the account when the lead converts. Account type and industry come from your own master lists, and you can add a missing value without leaving the form.

  • Attach the enquiry or visiting card
  • Source and campaign tracked
  • Assign in the same send

Follow-ups

Never lose a follow-up to someone's memory

Log the call, the meeting or the site visit with one line of outcome. The date and the person are filled in for you and cannot be backdated. Answer two questions and the lead gets a dated next step and a Pending task, both raised in the same send.

  • 7 activity types
  • Dated next step
  • Task raised automatically

Conversion

One send writes three records, with nothing retyped

Mark the lead Converted and pick the route. Neome writes the account, the contact person and the opportunity together, carrying the type, industry, phone and email across. Attach the deal to an existing account or let it create a new one.

  • Account + contact + opportunity
  • Or account and contact only
  • Lead id stays traceable

Pipeline

The whole pipeline on one board

Deals sit as cards under the stage they have actually reached, with the value and the owner on the face of each one. Drag a card to move the deal. Every column totals what it is holding, so the forecast updates itself.

  • Your own stages
  • Kanban or sheet view
  • Won, or a recorded lost reason

AI

Most CRMs wait to be filled. This one helps fill itself

An AI agent sources prospects that match the customers you already sell to, turns genuine inbound enquiries into leads, completes the detail that came in half-blank and scores the list. Then run an email campaign against those leads, once or on a schedule.

  • Tagged with the AI Agent source
  • Scored before anyone calls
  • One-time or scheduled sends

Enquiries arriving on WhatsApp can feed the same queue. WhatsApp Inquiry Management syncs the groups and chats you choose and tells a real enquiry from chatter.

Migration

Bring the spreadsheet you already have

Pick your Excel file and map its headers onto CRM fields. Anything already named the same maps itself, mandatory fields are flagged before the import runs, and the row count in the sheet header confirms exactly what landed.

  • Column mapping
  • Mandatory fields flagged
  • Bulk-assign owners after import

Reporting

Turn what your team entered into a forecast

Set a date range and every figure below it is bounded by those dates.

New, open, follow-up and converted counters, the funnel by status, lead ageing, which channels convert, and how loaded each owner is.

Sales Executive Performance

One executive's leads, conversions, opportunities, open pipeline and revenue won, plus their activity mix and a worklist sorted by days open.

CRM Performance

Open pipeline, revenue won, conversion rate, value at every stage, value carried by each executive, ageing deals, and why deals were lost.
