How Tanya Collection Ltd brought jewellery manufacturing, CRM, and vendor operations onto Neome

Company

Tanya Collection Ltd is a fine diamond jewellery manufacturer based at the Gemopolis Industrial Estate in Bangkok, Thailand, established in 1997. It designs and manufactures jewellery for retailers and brands. Manufacturing planning, workers, vendors, customer relationships, repairs, and internal approvals had been tracked separately, and the company needed one platform aligned with how that jewellery workflow actually runs.

The challenge

Tanya Collection was running manufacturing planning, workers, vendors, customers, repairs, and internal approvals as separate processes. As the business grew, that made status hard to see and follow-up dependent on whoever last held the record.

  • Different teams kept their own records, with no shared view of the operation.
  • Manufacturing and FO planning needed tighter coordination.
  • Worker rejects and production activity had no structured tracking.
  • Vendor rates and vendor business lived outside a central record.
  • Customer and CRM interactions had no single history.
  • Repair and maintenance work lacked consistent follow-up.
  • Meetings, calendars, checklists, and internal complaints were hard to see.
  • Management had no real-time dashboard across these activities.

The Neome solution

Neome brought those activities onto one platform, with modules and workflows configured around Tanya Collection's jewellery manufacturing process.

CRM and jewellery catalogue

Customer records, interactions, CRM registers, customer labour, and the jewellery catalogue sit in one history the team can look up.

Manufacturing and worker management

FO plans, worker rejects, production checklists, and end-of-process (EOP) tracking give manufacturing a structured record from plan through completion.

Vendor management

Vendor rates, vendor business, and related activity are kept in one place for the teams that work with them.

Repair and maintenance

Repairs and maintenance are logged with status and follow-up, so open work stays visible.

Meetings, checklists, and complaints

Calendar items, meeting follow-ups, operational checklists, tickets, and internal complaints can be assigned, owned, and tracked through to resolution.

Rates and the management dashboard

FX position, vendor rates, and customer labour sit alongside a dashboard covering CRM, manufacturing, rejects, vendors, repairs, tickets, and meetings.

Key learnings

  • Jewellery manufacturing needs the platform shaped around FO plans, worker rejects, vendors, and repairs.
  • Central records for customers, vendors, and workers remove the need to rebuild history from separate files.
  • Assigning meetings, complaints, tickets, and repairs makes follow-up visible to the team that owns it.
  • A single dashboard is what lets management see CRM, manufacturing, vendors, and repairs together.
