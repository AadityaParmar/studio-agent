The challenge

Manual operations were creating bottlenecks across attendance, orders, production reporting, inventory, and follow-ups.

  • Manual attendance created inaccuracies, time theft, and payroll errors.
  • Order teams struggled with margin tracking, live order updates, purchase orders, dispatch reminders, and partial deliveries.
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly production reports took heavy manual effort.
  • Stock blind spots caused production delays when raw materials ran low unexpectedly.
  • Worker performance and productivity patterns were difficult to measure.
  • Lead and quotation follow-ups were inconsistent, creating missed opportunities.

The Neome solution

Neome connected manufacturing workflows into a single operational system with mobile access, automated calculations, and real-time reporting.

Smart attendance system

Mobile punch-in/out integrated with payroll to reduce manual errors and automate salary calculations.

Automated order processing

Profit margin tracking, live order updates, raw material purchase orders, and dispatch notifications.

Real-time production dashboard

Live production tracking on mobile with automated daily, weekly, and monthly reporting.

Intelligent inventory alerts

Automated low-stock notifications sent to responsible teams before shortages affect production.

Performance analytics

Person-wise and product-wise productivity tracking for clearer worker performance visibility.

Lead management

Lead tracking and automated quotation follow-ups so opportunities are not missed.
