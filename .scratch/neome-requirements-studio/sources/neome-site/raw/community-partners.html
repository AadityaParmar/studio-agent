What each side does

The sales path leaves delivery with Neome. The implementation path keeps delivery with you.

Sales partner

You

  • Identify prospects in your network
  • Follow up and keep the conversation moving
  • Arrange demos and meetings
  • Stay with the customer until a decision
  • Help with payment follow-up

Neome

  • Requirement analysis
  • Solution design, configuration, and customization
  • Customer onboarding and training
  • Support after go-live

Implementation partner

You

  • Identify prospects and follow the conversation through to a decision
  • Gather requirements and document the workflow
  • Configure Neome for the customer
  • Train users and take the customer live
  • Provide first-line support after go-live

Neome

  • The product and approved product information
  • Help on product questions that sit outside the project
  • Written approval before pricing, discounts, scope, or any commitment that binds Neome

You stay independent on either path. You are not an employee or an agent of Neome, and you cannot bind Neome unless we agree to that in writing.

How it works

The same four steps for both paths. Onboarding differs once you are in.

STEP 1

Tell us about your practice

Share who you are, the businesses you can introduce, and whether you want to sell, implement, or both.

STEP 2

We talk and share the proposal

We connect, answer questions, and send the written proposal for the path you choose.

STEP 3

Sign the agreement

Both sides sign the Community Partner Agreement once the terms are comfortable.

STEP 4

We onboard you

Sales partners get a product briefing and lead registration. Implementation partners get that, plus delivery enablement so they can run a project. Then you start introducing customers.
