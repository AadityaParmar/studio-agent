Neome vs Vyapar: Billing App or Business Platform?

Neome vs Vyapar compared for Indian small businesses: a full chat-first operations platform with CRM, field teams, and 25+ modules vs Vyapar's GST billing, accounting, and inventory app.

Vyapar is a popular Indian billing, accounting, and inventory app, purpose-built for small shops and traders who need GST-compliant invoices and simple books. For that job, it is fast, affordable, and works offline.

Neome is a different category of tool: a chat-first business platform with 25+ modules spanning CRM, sales, field service, HR, operations, and inventory. This page compares the two so you can see where Vyapar is the right fit and where a growing team outgrows a billing app.

The short answer

Choose Neome if you need to run operations across sales, field, service, and people rather than just send invoices. Choose Vyapar if your main need is GST billing, accounting, and basic inventory for a small shop.

Billing tool vs operations platform

Vyapar does one job well: GST invoices, accounting, and stock for a shop. If that is all you need, it is hard to beat on price and simplicity.

Neome runs the whole business (leads and pipeline, field teams, service tickets, approvals, HR, and inventory) from WhatsApp, chat, Excel, and the web, with everything sharing one dataset.

When you outgrow billing software

As a business adds sales reps, field staff, service workflows, and approvals, a billing app's scope runs out, and data ends up scattered across WhatsApp, Excel, and the invoicing tool.

Neome is built for exactly that moment: structured workflows and dashboards across teams, with live location tracking for field forces that Vyapar does not offer.

Pricing in context

Vyapar's annual licence (from ₹599/year) is very economical for a single user or a small shop focused on billing.

Neome is priced per user per month (₹299$9.99) because it is a multi-user team platform. The cost reflects 25+ modules and team-wide automation, not just invoicing.

Where Vyapar is stronger

  • Purpose-built, very affordable GST billing and accounting for small shops and traders.
  • Simple and focused, with a minimal learning curve for invoicing and books.
  • Strong offline desktop billing.
  • Low annual cost for a single-user, billing-only use case.

Which should you choose?

Choose Neome if…

SMEs that need to coordinate sales, field teams, service, and operations instead of just generating invoices.

Choose Vyapar if…

Small shops and traders whose main need is GST billing, accounting, and basic inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Is Neome an alternative to Vyapar?+

They serve different needs. Vyapar is a billing and accounting app; Neome is a full operations platform with CRM, field service, HR, and 25+ modules. Neome is the better fit once you need more than invoicing.

Does Neome do GST billing like Vyapar?+

Neome is GST/PAN-aware and handles accounting across the business. For pure shop billing, Vyapar is simpler; for teams running sales, field, and operations, Neome does far more.

Which is cheaper, Neome or Vyapar?+

Vyapar (from ₹599/year) is cheaper for a single-user, billing-only shop. Neome is priced per user per month because it is a multi-user platform covering 25+ modules.

Can Neome track field staff like a billing app can't?+

Yes. Neome includes built-in live location tracking for field teams and customer workflows, which Vyapar does not offer.

Does Neome work offline?+

Yes. Like Vyapar, Neome works both online and offline.
