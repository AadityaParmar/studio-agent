Company

Gallon Water manufactures and distributes packaged drinking water across production plants and warehouses. As the business scaled, order fulfillment, inventory, dispatch, payments, and HR needed to operate as one connected system instead of separate manual workflows.

The challenge

As Gallon Water expanded, orders, production, inventory, dispatches, payments, and employees were managed through manual processes and spreadsheets, which limited visibility and slowed operations.

  • No centralized way to see whether an order was pending approval, warehouse-assigned, inventory-allocated, loaded, or dispatched.
  • Orders got stuck at process stages with no aging visibility or accountability.
  • Operations teams manually followed up across warehouse, dispatch, and sales to move orders forward.
  • Old inactive orders stayed open and distorted production planning and inventory allocation.
  • Customers with overdue balances could still place new orders, increasing collection risk.
  • Inventory across plants and warehouses required manual reconciliation for current, opening, and closing stock.
  • Order processing, production, inventory, dispatch, payments, and HR worked as disconnected systems.

The Neome solution

Neome delivered a fully integrated ERP and HRMS platform that automated order lifecycle, reminders, credit control, inventory movements, and management visibility.

Automated order lifecycle

Orders move automatically from creation through approval, warehouse assignment, inventory allocation, loading, dispatch, and delivery based on actual activity in the system.

Reminder and escalation engine

Automated reminders notify responsible teams when orders stay pending beyond defined timelines, preventing bottlenecks.

Automatic order cancellation

Orders inactive for more than 7 days are cancelled by business rules so planning and reporting stay accurate.

Customer credit control

Outstanding balances are monitored continuously, and overdue customers can be blocked from placing new orders until approval requirements are met.

Real-time inventory management

Stock updates from production, material transfers, FG transfers, dispatches, and adjustments give plant-wise and warehouse-wise visibility instantly.

Centralized business dashboard

Management views orders, production, inventory, dispatches, payments, and employee activity from one operational dashboard.
