Somewhere in that group chat is a customer waiting for a reply

Small and medium businesses live inside WhatsApp: trade groups, supplier circles, customer chats. That's also where the chaos lives.

Buried

Enquiries get lost

A real order sits unread between forty messages of chatter.

Unowned

No one is clearly on it

Anyone might reply, or nobody does, and you find out days later.

Forgotten

No record once it scrolls

No trail of what was asked, promised, or followed up.

Neome closes all three by reading the groups you choose, telling an enquiry apart from the chatter, and giving every one of them a status.

Setup

Keep the number your customers already message

Neome links to your existing WhatsApp the same way WhatsApp Web does. Scan once and you are connected, with no new number and no waiting on API approvals. Then choose exactly what it listens to: all your groups, hand-picked ones, or none, with one-to-one chats controlled separately.

  • No new number
  • Per-group control
  • Change it anytime

AI

The difference between an order and small talk

Every incoming message is read and tagged automatically as an Inquiry or General chatter. Your team's queue only ever surfaces what actually needs a response, so a genuine customer question never hides behind two hundred members saying good morning.

  • Tagged on arrival
  • Inquiry or General
  • Nothing to configure

Reply

Answer from Neome, arrive on WhatsApp

Send a reply from the unified inbox and it is delivered as an ordinary WhatsApp message from your business number. Nothing looks different to the customer, and your team never opens the app or passes a phone around to answer.

  • From your business number
  • One shared inbox
  • Unlimited seats

Workflow

Every enquiry has a status, not just a timestamp

Move each one through New, In Progress and Closed so nothing quietly falls through. You can see what is still open at a glance, and the record survives long after the chat has scrolled past it.

  • New → In Progress → Closed
  • Open queue at a glance
  • A record that persists
