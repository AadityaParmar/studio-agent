Company

260 Brands is an African consumer goods manufacturer based in the Chinika Industrial Area, Lusaka. It manufactures, markets, and distributes food and beverage brands including Golden Goodness, Emilios, Thobwa, HEPS, and Nutrameal, all made from locally sourced ingredients supplied by local suppliers and smallholder farmers. Its route to market runs through field sales teams, traditional retail beats, modern trade accounts, and tricycle riders serving last-mile outlets.

The challenge

Field execution, stock held by sales executives, modern trade accounts, collections, and quality complaints were tracked across phone calls, spreadsheets, and paper, leaving management with little reliable visibility into what happened each day. Much of that work happens on beats through remote areas where mobile network is weak or missing entirely.

  • No dependable record of whether field teams reported for work or worked their assigned beats.
  • Sales visits were reported verbally, with no way to confirm an outlet had actually been visited.
  • Orders captured on paper and over calls were slow to process and easy to get wrong.
  • Stock issued to sales executives could not be reconciled against what they sold or returned.
  • Beat and route plans lived on paper, so coverage gaps only surfaced after the fact.
  • Modern trade accounts needed different handling from traditional retail but ran through the same informal process.
  • Quality complaints from the market were collected informally and often lost before resolution.
  • Sales targets, achievement, and outstanding collections were reconciled by hand at month end.
  • Sales teams travel daily to remote areas with little or no mobile network, so nothing could be recorded at the point of the visit.
  • The field team is not highly technical, so anything that looked like conventional sales software risked poor adoption.

The Neome solution

Neome gave 260 Brands native Android and iOS apps with a chat-based interface and full offline support, so attendance, beats, visits, orders, executive stock, collections, and complaints run as connected workflows whether or not the team has a signal, with real-time dashboards for sales management.

Team attendance

Time-bound attendance capture with GPS verification gives supervisors an accurate daily record across field and depot teams.

Beat planning and rider routes

Daily beats and routes are assigned and tracked for sales executives, with tricycle rider routes covering last-mile outlets.

Sales visits and quality complaints

Visits are GPS-tagged with role-specific forms and photo capture, and quality complaints raised in the market are logged with evidence and tracked to closure.

Orders across traditional and modern trade

Field orders from retail beats and modern trade accounts are captured digitally through workflows suited to each channel.

Executive stock request and assignment

Warehouse inventory connects to per-executive stock through a request-and-assign flow, so what each executive carries is reconciled against sales and returns.

Targets, collections, and ledger

Sales targets and achievement track against live activity, while payment collection entries post to a customer ledger instead of month-end reconciliation.

Full offline mode on native apps

Native Android and iOS apps let the team record attendance, visits, orders, stock, and collections with no connection at all. Anything submitted or edited offline syncs automatically once the device is back on a network.

Chat-based interface

The apps work through a familiar chat-style interface, so a non-technical field team picks the platform up quickly instead of being trained on conventional sales software.

Key learnings

  • A field tool has to keep working with no signal and sync later, or remote beats simply go unrecorded.
  • A chat-based interface let a non-technical sales team adopt the platform with very little training.
  • GPS verification on attendance and visits turns field reporting into evidence rather than recollection.
  • Traditional beats and modern trade need distinct order workflows, but they belong on the same platform.
  • Tracking stock in the hands of sales executives closes the gap between dispatch and actual sales.
  • Capturing quality complaints in the field, with photos, protects the brand before issues spread.
  • Linking targets and collections to daily activity removes the month-end reconciliation scramble.
