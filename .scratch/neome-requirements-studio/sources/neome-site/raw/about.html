Why Neome exists

The system we wished we had, built for real-world execution.

Neome is a conversational automation platform designed to work for one person or a team of ten thousand. It's a consumer-enterprise hybrid, with consumer-grade simplicity and enterprise-level power.

Our focus is simple: deliver powerful, ready-to-deploy systems that are practical, scalable, and built for real-world execution. Start with what you need and expand seamlessly as you grow.

Neome =AI ( Messaging + Spreadsheet + Drive )

What makes Neome different

Built on four ideas the enterprise world keeps getting wrong.

Beyond privacy, security, governance, and compliance, Neome is built on the foundations that growing teams actually need.

01

Accessibility

An intuitive, universal interface that needs no training. Work where your team already lives, in messaging, spreadsheets, and drives.

02

Affordability

Real-time interactivity with phased adoption. No disruptive integrations and no rip-and-replace, so you can start small and grow.

03

Pluggability

Effortless integration through ready-to-use plugins for the SaaS apps you already rely on. Connect what you have, extend as you go.

04

AI Native

An LLM reasoning engine embedded into every layer instead of bolted on. Automate decisions, summaries, and follow-ups across your workflows.
