A rental fleet does not lose money on the road. It loses it in the office.

Three gaps account for most of what a growing fleet leaks, and none of them is a vehicle problem.

Onboarding

A day lost before the first mile

Licence photos on WhatsApp, the agreement in a drawer, the deposit in a notebook. Nobody can say who is cleared to ride.

Collection

Rent you chase instead of collect

A hundred drivers on daily rent means a hundred reminders, and the shortfall only shows up at month end.

Downtime

Vehicles off the road, nobody knows why

A complaint becomes a phone call, the phone call becomes a repair, and the cost never reaches the vehicle it belongs to.

Neome closes all three by keeping one record per driver and one per vehicle, and making the money, the mileage and the maintenance hang off them.

Onboarding

A driver cleared to ride, with nothing left in a drawer

Capture the licence, the address proof and the signed agreement against the driver record, take the security deposit, and attach the plan they are on. The vehicle cannot be handed over until the checklist is complete, so an unverified driver never leaves the branch on your asset.

  • Documents with expiry dates
  • Deposit and refund tracked
  • Deboarding in one step

Fleet

Every vehicle is on rent, available, or in the workshop

One register per branch holds the chassis, registration, battery or engine, purchase date and insurance for each vehicle, and its live status. Allocate a vehicle to a driver, swap it when one goes in for service, and take it back on deboarding. The history stays on the vehicle.

  • Allocation history per vehicle
  • Insurance and document expiry
  • Branch-wise availability

Collection

The rent collects itself, every morning

Each driver carries a prepaid wallet. The daily plan debits automatically, a weekly or monthly plan does the same on its own cycle, and a top-up made from the driver app lands in the same balance. You stop raising invoices and start reading a balance.

  • Daily, weekly or monthly plans
  • Low-balance alerts
  • Full statement per driver

IoT

Unpaid rent stops the next start, not your day

Connect the telematics unit already fitted to the vehicle and you get live location, odometer and battery on the same screen as the rental. Tie the start permission to the wallet: when the balance runs out, the next start is blocked until it is topped up. A vehicle already moving is never cut off.

  • Live location and odometer
  • Start tied to wallet balance
  • Works with your hardware

Maintenance

Every complaint becomes a job card, every job card a cost

A driver raises a complaint from their phone. It becomes a job card at the branch workshop, spares are issued against it from your own stores, and the labour and parts are booked to the vehicle. Rent pauses while the vehicle is off the road, so the driver is not charged for your downtime.

  • Complaint to job card
  • Spare parts issued from stock
  • Cost per vehicle

Reporting

Run five branches the way you ran one

Set a date range and every figure below it is bounded by those dates.

Multi-branch

Who owns what?

Each branch carries its own fleet, drivers, workshop and stores. Branch managers see their own operation; head office sees all of them side by side.

Utilisation and revenue

Is the fleet earning?

Vehicles on rent against vehicles owned, revenue per vehicle, idle days, and the drivers whose wallets keep running dry.

Cost of running

What is it costing?

Service history, repeat faults, spares consumed and the maintenance cost carried by each vehicle over its life.
