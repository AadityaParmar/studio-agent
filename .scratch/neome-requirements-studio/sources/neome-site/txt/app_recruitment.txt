Most hiring is not lost at the interview. It is lost in the gaps.
Three gaps account for most of what a growing team loses in a search, and none of them is a judgement problem.
Scattered
Candidates live in three inboxes
CVs arrive by email and sit with whoever received them, so nobody can say who has applied or where they got to.
Unstructured
Every interviewer asks something different
Without shared criteria, two panels produce two impressions that cannot be compared or defended.
Slow
The good candidate accepted elsewhere
An offer waited days for an approval nobody was chasing, and the market does not wait with it.
Neome closes all three by budgeting the role, keeping one pipeline, and moving the offer while it still matters.

A role opened against a budget, not a wish
A position is linked to its department and carries the budgeted salary band from the moment it opens. Agreeing the band before interviewing rather than after is what stops a hiring process ending in a candidate you cannot afford and a conversation nobody enjoys.
