Reactive
Maintenance happens when something stops
Servicing is remembered under pressure, so the plant runs on repairs rather than on a plan.
Unexplained
The same fault, again

Uncounted
Nobody knows what a machine costs to keep
Labour and parts are absorbed into overheads, so replacing an asset is argued rather than calculated.
Neome closes all three by planning on usage, recording the cause, and counting what each asset costs.
Preventive
Maintenance triggered by the calendar or by the hours run
A plan fires on a date or on usage, whichever comes first, and raises the task with its checklist and a named technician. Servicing on running hours rather than the calendar alone is what stops a lightly used machine being over-serviced and a hard-worked one being missed.

Breakdowns
Record the downtime and the reason, not just the repair
A breakdown raises a work order that captures when the line stopped, how long it was down and what actually caused it. Root cause is a field rather than a conversation, which is the only way a repeating fault becomes visible as a pattern instead of a run of separate incidents.
