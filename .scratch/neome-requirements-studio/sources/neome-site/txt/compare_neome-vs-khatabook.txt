Neome vs Khatabook: Digital Ledger or Business Platform?
Neome vs Khatabook compared: a full chat-first business platform with CRM, field teams, and 25+ modules vs Khatabook's free digital ledger (bahi khata) for tracking transactions and credit.
Khatabook is a free digital ledger (bahi khata) app, hugely popular with Indian micro and small businesses for recording transactions and tracking customer credit (udhaar). It is simple, mobile-first, and free to start.
Neome is a full chat-first business platform with 25+ modules covering CRM, sales, field service, HR, operations, and inventory. The two solve very different problems, so this page makes the distinction clear.
The short answer
Choose Neome if you run a team-based business that needs CRM, workflows, and field operations. Choose Khatabook if all you need is a simple, free digital ledger to track customer credit and daily transactions.

Digital ledger vs business platform
Khatabook is built around one thing: a simple digital ledger to record who owes what and to track daily cash and credit. For a solo owner or a kirana store, that is often exactly enough.
Neome is a full platform covering CRM, field service, HR, approvals, inventory, manufacturing, and more, operated from WhatsApp and the web. It manages the business, not just the ledger.
When a ledger app isn't enough
The moment a business adds staff, sales pipelines, field visits, or service workflows, a ledger app can no longer hold the operation together.
Neome gives those teams structure: assigned tasks, automated follow-ups, real-time dashboards, role-based access, and live location tracking for field staff, none of which a ledger app provides.
Free vs invested
Khatabook's free tier is excellent for a solo or micro business that just needs to track credit on a phone.
Neome is a paid, multi-user platform (with a 15-day free trial, no card). It is an investment that makes sense once a team needs to run real operations rather than just keep a ledger.
