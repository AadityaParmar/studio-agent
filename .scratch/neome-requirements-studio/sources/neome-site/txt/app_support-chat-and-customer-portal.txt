Support is slow for one reason: nobody has the context.
Three gaps account for most of the time a support desk loses, and none of them is an effort problem.
Repeated
The customer explains it again
A new person picks up the thread with none of the history, so the customer tells the story a third time.
Unrecorded
The chat ended, the problem did not
A promise made in a conversation leaves no ticket behind, so nothing chases it and nobody owns it.
Interrupting
Every status question is a phone call
Customers cannot see their own tickets or orders, so the only way to find out is to interrupt someone.
Neome closes all three by attaching the history, turning conversations into tickets, and letting customers look for themselves.
