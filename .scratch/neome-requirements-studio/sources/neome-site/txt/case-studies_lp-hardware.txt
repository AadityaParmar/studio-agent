The challenge
Disconnected systems across procurement, inventory, production, sales, finance, and HR created duplicate masters, inventory errors, and unfair order fulfillment.
Disconnected systems across procurement, inventory, production, sales, finance, and HR.
Duplicate and inconsistent master data living in spreadsheets.
Manual quotation and purchase order creation with frequent size and specification mismatches.
No QR-based inward tracking, lab test, or heat test before material approval.
Raw materials issued randomly instead of FIFO, with poor production visibility.
Sales dispatches ignored FIFO priority, so newer orders shipped ahead of older pending ones.
Payment tracking and field employee attendance, visits, and expenses ran in separate tools.
The Neome solution
Neome ERP became the single manufacturing platform, covering everything from masters and QR inward through QA, FIFO material issue, production, outbound dispatch, finance, and HRMS.
Centralized master data
Customer, vendor, product, specification, size, warehouse, employee, tax, and unit masters unified into one source of truth.
Digital sales and purchasing
PDF quotations with email and WhatsApp sharing, plus validated purchase orders with size and specification checks.
QR-based inward and inventory
Every inward batch gets a QR code for scan-based receiving, warehouse mapping, batch stock, and FIFO allocation.
Integrated quality control
QA inspection, lab test, heat test, and approval or rejection workflows keep defective material out of production.
FIFO production and dispatch
Raw materials issue to production on FIFO, finished goods stay QR-tracked, and sales orders ship oldest-first with priority approval overrides.
Finance and HRMS integration
Order-wise payments, aging, and reminders connect to sales, while attendance, GPS visits, expenses, and approvals run inside the same ERP.

Key learnings
QR batch tracking is the foundation for both inventory accuracy and material traceability.
Quality gates before stock acceptance prevent defects from entering production.
FIFO rules for materials and sales orders reduce waste and improve fairness for customers.
Connecting finance and HRMS to operations closes the last visibility gaps management needs to scale.
