Three ways training quietly stops working
None of them is about the quality of the material. They are about consistency, timing and evidence.
Inconsistent
Training depends on who ran it that day
When induction is a conversation, the second intake gets a shorter version of it and nobody notices for a year.
Forgotten
Onboarding starts when somebody remembers
A new joiner's first course arrives whenever an email gets sent, which is usually after the week in which it would have mattered.
Unverifiable
You cannot prove who was trained
A signature on a sheet in a drawer answers no question a customer, an auditor or an incident review will ask.
Neome fixes all three by fixing the course, assigning it from the joining date, and keeping the record.
