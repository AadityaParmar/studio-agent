Hi Asha,

As promised after Monday's call, here is how expense claims should work. I handle Accounts at Fernbrook.

Today staff fill in a paper form and put the receipts in an envelope on my desk. Receipts get lost and I chase people every month.

What we want:

1. Staff submit a claim from their phone: date, category (Travel, Meals or Site materials), amount in euro, a short description and a photo of the receipt.
2. Their line manager approves or rejects the claim. The claimant is told either way.
3. Every Friday I pay the approved claims by bank transfer from the bank's website, then mark them Paid in the new system. The bank stays as it is; I do the transfers myself.
4. We do not accept claims for spending more than 60 days old. The system should refuse them.
5. Maya and I want a report each month of spend by category.

Staff should see only their own claims. I see all of them.
