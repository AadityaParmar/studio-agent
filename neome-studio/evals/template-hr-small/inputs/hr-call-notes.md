# Call notes: leave at Fernbrook Interiors

Date: 2026-10-20
On the call: Maya Lindqvist (Owner and Managing Director), Jonas Petrov (HR Manager). For Neomenta: Asha Rao.

## Who we are

- Fernbrook Interiors designs and fits out offices and shops. 38 staff in two studios, Northgate and Riverside. Five line managers.
- Everything in English. Amounts in euro.

## Leave today

- Staff email their line manager to ask for leave. The manager replies by email. Jonas copies each request into a spreadsheet.
- Requests get lost in email. Managers approve two people from the same team for the same week without noticing.
- Leave types: Annual, Sick, Unpaid.

## What they want

- Staff ask for leave from their phone: leave type, from date, to date, reason.
- The line manager approves or rejects it. The employee is told the decision.
- Leave longer than 5 working days also needs Jonas (HR Manager) to approve, after the line manager.
- Sick leave longer than 2 days: the employee adds a photo of the doctor's note.
- Managers want a team calendar showing who is off, so they can spot clashes before they approve.
- Staff see only their own leave. Line managers see their team's leave. Jonas sees all leave.

## Payroll

- Salaries are run by Paystream Payroll, an outside payroll company. Fernbrook does not run payroll itself and will keep Paystream.
- Unpaid leave days must reach Paystream by the 25th of each month. Today Clara Osei (Accounts) retypes them from Jonas's spreadsheet into Paystream's upload sheet.
- Maya: "Unpaid days must flow from the new system to payroll. No more retyping."
