# Call notes: Marlin Marine Services, workshop

Date: 14 September 2026
On the call: Leena Joshi (Owner and Managing Director), Tomas Varga (Workshop Manager). Neomenta: Asha Rao.

## How work comes in today

- Boat owners phone or WhatsApp the front desk to book a service. The front desk (2 people) writes it in the "Jobs 2026" Excel sheet.
- Tomas: "A job is one boat coming into the workshop for a service or a repair."
- For each job we note: boat name, owner name and phone, engine make and model, what the owner says is wrong, and the date the boat comes in.
- Tomas gives each job to one of the 6 technicians.
- About 40 jobs a month.

## In the workshop

- The Excel status column today: Booked, In workshop, Waiting for parts, Done, Invoiced.
- Technicians write hours worked and parts used on a paper job card. The front desk copies it into Excel at the end of the day. Often late, sometimes lost.
- Leena: "We lose jobs in the sheet. Nobody knows which boats are stuck waiting for parts."
- Tomas wants one list of every boat waiting for parts, oldest first.

## When the job is done

- The front desk calls the owner to collect the boat. Leena wants the owner to get a WhatsApp message by itself when the job is Done.
- Omar (Accounts) makes the invoice in Tally from the job card. Invoices stay in Tally.

## Reports

- Leena wants every month: number of jobs per technician and hours per technician.

## Other

- Technicians should see only their own jobs. Leena sees everything.
- We have two years of jobs in Excel (the 2025 and 2026 sheets). Leena wants them in the new system so each boat's history is there.
