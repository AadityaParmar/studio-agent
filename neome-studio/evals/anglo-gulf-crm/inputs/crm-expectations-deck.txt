CRM Expectations
Condensed for management review
September 2026

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 - CUSTOMERS, LEADS AND THE SALES PIPELINE
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Why we need a CRM
- Customer data sits in personal Excel sheets, WhatsApp and email. When someone leaves, the customer history leaves with them.
- Management cannot see the pipeline without calling each salesperson.

Customer and contact management
- One record per customer (account)
- Customer type, industry, country, assigned salesperson
- Customer class A / B / C (A = critical)
- Multiple contacts per customer; mark the decision makers
- Credit terms visible on the customer
- Purchase history of the customer
- Last interaction and next follow-up always visible on the customer
- Complete customer view: leads, opportunities, quotations, visits, follow-ups all in one place

Lead management
- Every potential deal captured as a lead with owner + status
- Qualified lead converts to account and contact, keep the link to the lead

Sales pipeline  <<< MOST IMPORTANT PART >>>
- Full visibility from first contact to closed deal
- Stages: Lead > Qualification > Quotation > Negotiation > PO > Won / Lost
- Each opportunity: customer or lead, product, value, currency, expected close date, stage, owner, country
- Visual pipeline (board view), count and value per stage
- Customer PO receipt recorded (PO reference + date)

Pipeline analytics
- Total pipeline, weighted pipeline, expected orders, actual orders, target - side by side
- Slice by country / salesperson / product / customer
- Expected orders in 30 / 60 / 90 day buckets
- Targets by salesperson, product and country
- Example value on slide: AED 1.2M weighted pipeline, Q4

Quotation tracking
- Quotation number, product, quantity, price, validity, approval status, linked opportunity
- Expected PO date
- Cost and gross margin on the quotation
- Revision history - keep every version

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 - FIELD WORK, FOLLOW-UPS, DASHBOARDS
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Customer visits and meetings
- Log every visit: customer, date, participants, purpose, discussion, linked opportunity,
  competitor information, next action, owner and deadline
- Field teams travel across Middle East, Africa and CIS and log visits from phone or tablet
- Management question: how many visits happened this week and what business came from them?

Discipline rule
- NO open opportunity without a next action, an owner and a deadline

Follow-ups and reminders (MINIMUM REQUIREMENT)
- Every follow-up has an owner and a deadline
- Automatic flags: RED = overdue, ORANGE = due today, YELLOW = due this week, GREEN = completed
- Reminder to the owner

Customer master again
- Customer type and industry must be filterable (same as slide 1)

Salesperson dashboard
- Target vs actual sales, achievement %, conversion ratio, new customers, opportunities,
  pipeline, weighted pipeline, visits, follow-ups, orders won and lost
- Collections and outstanding per salesperson
- Targets per salesperson

Product, geography and molecule view
- Product: oilfield, water treatment, agrochemicals
- Geography: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Africa, CIS, others
- Molecule: DBNPA, corrosion and scale inhibitors, biocides, others

Product and market intelligence
- Keep sales teams current on the market

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 - MANAGEMENT VIEW, MARKET, ERP
-----------------------------------------------------------------

One-page management dashboard
- Sales | Pipeline | Customers | Business Development | Collections | People
- Risks: lost orders, at-risk customers, delayed deals, overdue actions

Won / lost
- Every closed opportunity: Won or Lost, with the reason

Competitor tracking
- Competitor name, price, product, customer preference, competitive advantage,
  reason for winning or losing
- Build a competitor database over time

Registration and tender tracker
- Country, customer, product, registration, authority, submission date, status,
  expected approval, tender, value
- Example: Tanzania | Product Y | Approval Pending | Tender Q1 | USD 500K potential

ERP
- The CRM must NOT run standalone next to the ERP (we use Tally ERP)
- One flow: CRM > quotation > sales order > ERP > invoice > collection
- One shared customer master, no double entry

SLIDE 4 - MOBILE, COMMUNICATION, WHAT WE EXPECT FROM THE VENDOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Mobile
- Full functionality on phone and tablet for travelling teams:
  update visits, add opportunities, upload quotations and notes, set next actions,
  check customer history, reach contacts

Communication
- WhatsApp Business: customer conversations become part of the customer history
- Calendar sync
- Email integration (Outlook / Gmail)

Users
- Roles and permissions that match our sales organisation

What we expect from the vendor
- Process mapping
- Data migration
- Masters
- Roles
- Pipeline and dashboard configuration
- Integrations
- Training
- UAT
- Go-live
- Support

The CRM should be configured around our process, not the other way round.
