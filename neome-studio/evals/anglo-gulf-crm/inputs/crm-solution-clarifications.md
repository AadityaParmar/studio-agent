Neomenta - Crescent Gulf CRM: solution questions and answers
Call on 29 September 2026. Answers from Crescent Gulf sales leadership, typed up by Neomenta.

1. How does a lead become a customer? Can you work a deal before the lead is a customer?

A lead is qualified first, then converted to an account (customer) and contact. Yes - an opportunity can be opened on a lead that is not converted yet, or on an existing account. Once we get the customer PO it is recorded on the opportunity. Our sales flow ends in sales order, invoice and collection in Tally.

For opportunity stages the team uses: New, Contacted, Quote, Negotiations, Won or Lost.

2. What does A / B / C mean on the customer?

A = critical customer, B = medium, C = low. Every customer must have one of the three.

3. How many people will use the CRM?

To be confirmed - the sales head will send numbers.

4. What do you mean by product and market intelligence?

We want the sales team to capture buying signals and market updates so everyone stays current. Not only reports.

5. How do quotation approvals work today?

By discount %:
- below 10% - no approval
- 10 to 20% - Level 1
- 20 to 25% - Level 1 and Level 2
- above 25% - Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3
We want to be able to change these bands and levels later without IT.

6. Which lead statuses do you use?

Sales head's list: New, Contacted, Qualified, On hold, Opportunity grooming, Closed (Disqualified or Converted).
The regional team's sheet uses: New, Contacted, On hold, Qualified, Disqualified, Converted.
A closed lead is always either Disqualified or Converted. We should be able to add or rename statuses ourselves.

For opportunities the regional sheet has a different list: Qualified, Requirement Study, Demo or Presentation, Won, Lost.
Stage changes should be traceable - who moved it and when.
