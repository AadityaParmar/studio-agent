Neomenta internal - Crescent Gulf CRM - scope decisions
29 September 2026, after the clarifications call.

Quotations
- The quotation document itself is made outside Neome (their template) and attached to the quotation record. Neome will not generate the quotation document or PDF.
- Expected PO date: field on the quotation, on Neome.
- Cost and gross margin come from Tally, not keyed on Neome.
- Each revision = new document added to the quotation. Never overwrite the old ones.

Pipeline and targets
- Weighted pipeline: logic to be agreed with the Crescent Gulf business. Do not invent it.
- Targets held on Neome.

Dashboards
- Collections and outstanding per salesperson: picked up from Tally.
- Management dashboard: Neomenta proposes the first set; further dashboards are defined with the business later.

Competitor database, registration and tender tracker
- Capture them as Crescent Gulf described them. Scope stays open until they answer our questions.

Communication
- WhatsApp Business API: Crescent Gulf procures it, or it is charged on actuals.
- Calendar sync: delivered on Neome.
- Email (Outlook / Gmail): part of the ERP integration scope, not ours.

ERP
- ERP integration is scoped separately with their Tally partner. Partner shares scope after discussion.
- Minimum data from Tally into Neome: quotation cost and gross margin, collections and outstanding.

Not discussed yet: users count, languages, hosting, data migration volumes, support terms.
