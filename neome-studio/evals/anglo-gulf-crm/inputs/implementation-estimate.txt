Neomenta implementation estimate - Crescent Gulf CRM
29 Sep 2026
Standard configuration on Neome. 4 weeks start to go-live.

Week 1 - master data and CRM configuration (masters, users and roles, core CRM records). Masters loaded by end of week 1.
Week 2 - workflow and process configuration (stages, approvals, reminders, pipeline and dashboards).
Week 3 - testing, fixes, final adjustments. UAT with Crescent Gulf.
Week 4 - go-live and change management. Training is part of change management, for users in each role.

Not in the 4 weeks: process mapping timing, data migration, post go-live support, ERP / email integration (Tally partner).
