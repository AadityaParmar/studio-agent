- Lead fields — Mitesh (Owner): Lead fields are shop name, owner name, phone, address, GEO, category, notes.

- Conversion — Sales Head: A Converted Lead becomes a Customer through a Change Request.
- Change Request — Mitesh (Owner): A Change Request is when a Sales Agent asks to change customer details (address, phone, GEO); the Sales Supervisor approves; then it is pushed to SAP.
- New customers — Mitesh (Owner): New customers need Sales Supervisor approval before they go to SAP. A customer added in neome gets a temp code until SAP returns the CardCode.
- Goal — Sales Head: Every Lead is recorded with a Sales Agent and followed up; Leads converted to Customers are counted every month; no Leads kept in notebooks or WhatsApp.
- Pain today — Sales Head: Leads are kept in notebooks and WhatsApp; no record of follow-up; nobody counts how many become customers.
- Who adds Leads — Mitesh (Owner): Sales Agents only, not Tricycle Riders.
- Who sees the Lead Sheet/Report — Sales Head: Sales Agent sees own Leads; Sales Supervisor and above see all.
- Category — Mitesh (Owner): Same list as Customer Category: Retail Shop, Wholesaler, Supermarket, School, Institution.
- Final statuses — Sales Head: Converted and Lost are final; the Sales Agent moves a Lead; a Lost Lead is not reopened, the agent adds a new Lead.
- Offline — Mitesh (Owner): Sales Agent adds and updates Leads offline; sync when online.
- Integrations — Mitesh (Owner): None of its own; a Converted Lead reaches SAP only as a new Customer through the Change Request.
- Notifications — Sales Head: No extra push; only the push on a Change Request decision.
- Volumes — Sales Head: About 60 Sales Agents; about 20 new Leads a day in total.
