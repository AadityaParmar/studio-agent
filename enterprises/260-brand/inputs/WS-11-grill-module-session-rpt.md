Only what the Enterprise said. Consultant's own views are proposals + Open Items, not recorded here.

- Purpose and KPI — Sales Head: Managers get every report from neome in minutes with no Excel made by hand; targets set before the month starts; incentive worked out in neome with no disputes.
- Pain today — Sales Head: Reports built by hand in Excel from paper and SAP; late; targets and incentives kept on paper; no view of debt or where customers are.
- Roles and headcount — Mitesh (Owner), OI-06: 1 Sales Head, 1 Owner, 2 Regional Sales Managers, 3 Area Sales Managers, 8 Sales Supervisors.
- Access scope — Mitesh (Owner), OI-02, OI-03: Regional Sales Manager sees reports for own region; Area Sales Manager own area; Sales Head sees all and sets targets. Sales Supervisor sees own agents.
- Who does not see reports — Mitesh (Owner): Sales Agents and Tricycle Riders see no reports, only their own entries in the sheets of other modules. Riders see no targets.
- Report list — Mitesh (Owner), OI-53: Daily Sales (by agent, beat, product; Supervisor and above), Attendance (Supervisor and above), Visit Productivity (Supervisor and above), Stock in hand (Supervisor and above), Debt vs Collection (Area Sales Manager and above), Target vs Achievement (all managers), Leave register (Supervisor and above), Complaints aging (Owner, Sales Head), Marketing Visits (Sales Head). Filters: date, area, supervisor, agent, beat, product. Export to Excel.
- Productive call — Mitesh (Owner), OI-54: Productive = a visit with an order. Agreed.
- Non-productive — Sales Head: A visit with no order is non-productive; the reason comes from the No-order reason list.
- Targets — Mitesh (Owner), OI-55: Sales Head sets monthly targets per Sales Agent, by value (ZMW) and by quantity per product group.
- Product group — Sales Head: Product group is the Category from the Category Master.
- Achievement — Sales Head: Sales value is the Invoiced and Delivered orders of the agent in the month, before VAT; Cancelled orders not counted.
- Target change — Sales Head: Sales Head can change a target until month end; the month-end incentive uses the target as at month end.
- No target — Sales Head: An agent with no target shows "No target" and gets no incentive.
- Incentive — Mitesh (Owner), OI-56: 2% of monthly sales value if target 100% or more achieved; 1% if 80 to 99%; none below 80%. Worked out in a neome report; paid through payroll outside neome.
- Incentive audience — Sales Head: Sales Head, Owner, Area and Regional Sales Managers see it for their people; a Sales Agent sees own target and incentive only.
- Debt — Mitesh (Owner), OI-57: Debt is SAP open balances plus neome credit invoices minus collections.
- Heat map — Mitesh (Owner), OI-58: Map of customers coloured by last-30-days sales value.
- Heat map detail — Sales Head: Customers without GEO are not shown; clicking a customer shows name, Category and last-30-days sales; Sales Supervisor and above.
- Daily summary — Sales Head: Daily Sales for yesterday shows at the top of the dashboard; no email, no push.
- Dashboards — Sales Head: A Performance dashboard (sales vs target, incentive earned) and an Efficiency dashboard (productive calls %, visits per agent per day) for Sales Supervisor and above; same filters.
- Offline — Sales Head: No; reports need network.
- Freshness — Mitesh (Owner): Neome figures show as soon as phones sync; SAP open balances up to 30 minutes old.
- Integrations — Mitesh (Owner): Only read SAP open balances through the sync (OI-11); nothing sent to SAP; Excel export only. SAP owner Chanda Mwila (OI-74).
- Volumes — Mitesh (Owner), OI-06: about 1,200 visits and 700 orders a day; 15 manager users. Report views per day: Enterprise did not know.
- Priority — Sales Head: Yes to all Requirements as proposed.
