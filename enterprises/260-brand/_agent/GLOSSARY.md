# 260 Brands

## Language

**Owner**:
User with full access to all modules, analytics and settings; adds masters.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Supervisor**:
Assigns beats and products to field staff, manages orders, monitors performance.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Employee**:
Staff member who clocks in/out, applies for leave and manages assigned inventory.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Agent**:
Field staff who places orders, collects payments, generates leads, logs visits and complaints.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Tricycle Rider**:
Field staff who records marketing visits only.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Area Sales Manager**:
Manager with full access to all modules.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Regional Sales Manager**:
Sales manager role; access not yet defined.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Head**:
Sales head role; access not yet defined.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Route**:
A sales route; each Beat sits on one Route.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Beat**:
A sales area on a Route; customers and employees are assigned to Beats for orders and visits.
_Source_: RFP-1, IN-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Employee - Beat Mapping**:
The Beats a Sales Supervisor has assigned to an employee.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer GEO Tagging**:
Recording a customer's map location in the app.
_Source_: RFP-1, IN-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer Code**:
A customer's code in the Customer Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**TPIN**:
A customer's TPIN number in the Customer Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Case Size**:
A product's case size, kept in the Product Master and Competitor Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Agent Inventory**:
Products a Sales Supervisor has assigned to a Sales Agent to sell from.
_Avoid_: Assign Inventory, Stock Assignment
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Stock Return**:
Return of stock; process not yet defined.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Visit Book**:
Record of a customer visit by sales or marketing staff, with an optional order and payment.
_Avoid_: Daily Visits
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Marketing Visit**:
A visit recorded by marketing field staff, with visit type and customer details.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**School Visit**:
A visit to a school, recording its procurement officer, head teacher and number of children.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Farm Visit**:
A visit to a farm, recording the person met and family size.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Door to door Visit**:
A household visit, recording the person met and family size.
_Avoid_: D2D
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Modern Trade**:
Daily update for a modern trade Store: shelf photos, stock count, competitor activities, supervisor review.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Store**:
A modern trade store in the Store Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Closing Stock Inventory**:
Stock per product per Store from Modern Trade stock counts.
_Avoid_: Closing stock
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Competitor**:
A competing brand and its products, kept in the Competitor Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer Complaint**:
A customer's product quality complaint logged by an agent for resolution tracking.
_Avoid_: Complain Ticket, Customer Quality Complaint
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Lead**:
A possible new customer generated by a Sales Agent.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Productive call**:
A visit counted as productive in call reports; definition not yet given.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Notice Board**:
In-app board for notices.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Weekly Beat Plan**:
Which Beat an employee works on each weekday, Monday to Saturday, made by the Sales Supervisor.
_Source_: WS-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Arrival Status**:
Marks a clock in as OnTime or Late. Start 08:00, grace 15 min; after 08:15 is Late.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Overtime**:
Hours worked after 17:00 on weekdays or on Sunday. The Employee requests it; the Sales Supervisor approves.
_Source_: WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Missed Clock-out**:
Mark on a day the Employee did not clock out; the app clocks out at 23:59 and the Sales Supervisor fixes the time.
_Source_: WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops
