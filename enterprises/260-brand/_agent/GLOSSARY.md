# 260 Brands

## Language

**Owner**:
User with full access to all modules, analytics and settings; adds masters.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Supervisor**:
Assigns beats and products to field staff, manages orders, monitors performance.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Employee**:
Staff member who clocks in/out, applies for leave and manages assigned inventory.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Agent**:
Field staff who places orders, collects payments, generates leads, logs visits and complaints.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Tricycle Rider**:
Field staff who records marketing visits only.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Area Sales Manager**:
Manager with full access to all modules.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Regional Sales Manager**:
Sales manager role; access not yet defined.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Head**:
Sales head role; access not yet defined.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Route**:
A sales route; each Beat sits on one Route.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Beat**:
A sales area on a Route; customers and employees are assigned to Beats for orders and visits.
_Source_: RFP-1, IN-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Employee - Beat Mapping**:
The Beats a Sales Supervisor has assigned to an employee.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer GEO Tagging**:
Recording a customer's map location in the app.
_Source_: RFP-1, IN-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer Code**:
A customer's code in the Customer Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**TPIN**:
A customer's TPIN number in the Customer Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Case Size**:
A product's case size, kept in the Product Master and Competitor Master.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Sales Agent Inventory**:
Products a Sales Supervisor has assigned to a Sales Agent to sell from; orders reduce it.
_Avoid_: Assign Inventory, Stock Assignment
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-4 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Stock Return**:
Unsold stock a Sales Agent returns at end of day; the Sales Supervisor counts it at the depot and accepts it (Pending, Accepted).
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-4 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Visit Book**:
Record of a customer visit by sales or marketing staff, with an optional order and payment.
_Avoid_: Daily Visits
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Marketing Visit**:
A visit recorded by a Tricycle Rider (or Sales Agent) for sampling and brand awareness, with Visit type and customer details.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-7 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**School Visit**:
A visit to a school, recording its procurement officer, head teacher and number of children.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Farm Visit**:
A visit to a farm, recording the person met and family size.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Door to door Visit**:
A household visit, recording the person met and family size.
_Avoid_: D2D
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Visit type**:
The kind of Marketing Visit: School, Farm, Door to door or Shop activation.
_Source_: WS-7 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**D2D - Nurtamilk**:
A door to door campaign for the Nurtamilk brand.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-7 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Modern Trade**:
Daily update for a modern trade Store: shelf photos, stock count, competitor activities, supervisor review.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-8 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Store**:
A modern trade store; also a Customer (Category Supermarket) flagged "Modern Trade".
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-8 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Closing Stock Inventory**:
Stock per product per Store from Modern Trade stock counts.
_Avoid_: Closing stock
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-8 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Competitor**:
A competing brand and its products, kept in the Competitor Master by the Sales Supervisor.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-8 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Customer Complaint**:
A customer's product quality complaint logged by an agent for resolution tracking.
_Avoid_: Complain Ticket, Customer Quality Complaint
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-9 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Change Request**:
A Sales Agent's request to change customer details (address, phone, GEO) or add a new Customer; the Sales Supervisor approves; then it is pushed to SAP.
_Source_: WS-6 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Lead**:
A possible new customer generated by a Sales Agent. Status: New, Contacted, Interested, Converted, Lost.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-6 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Productive call**:
A visit counted as productive in call reports; definition not yet given.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Notice Board**:
In-app board for notices.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Weekly Beat Plan**:
Which Beat an employee works on each weekday, Monday to Saturday, made by the Sales Supervisor.
_Source_: WS-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Arrival Status**:
Marks a clock in as OnTime or Late. Start 08:00, grace 15 min; after 08:15 is Late.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Overtime**:
Hours worked after 17:00 on weekdays or on Sunday. The Employee requests it; the Sales Supervisor approves.
_Source_: WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Missed Clock-out**:
Mark on a day the Employee did not clock out; the app clocks out at 23:59 and the Sales Supervisor fixes the time.
_Source_: WS-2 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Leave Balance**:
Days of Casual Leave or Sick Leave an Employee has left in the year. Casual 12 and Sick 10 days credited 1 January; Casual carries forward max 5.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Unpaid Leave**:
Leave days beyond the Leave Balance; allowed.
_Source_: WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Holiday List**:
Yearly list of Zambian public holidays kept by the Owner; not counted in leave.
_Source_: WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Depot Stock**:
Warehouse stock per product at the depot, read from SAP every 30 minutes; no warehouse transactions are kept in the app.
_Source_: WS-4 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Stock in hand**:
Report of what each Sales Agent holds per product: assigned, sold, returned, in hand.
_Source_: WS-4 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Van sale**:
An order the Sales Agent delivers from the van at once: Placed, Invoiced and Delivered in the same visit.
_Source_: WS-5 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**No-order reason**:
Reason a Sales Agent picks for a visit with no order: Shop closed, Stock enough, No money, Owner not there, Other.
_Source_: WS-5 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Invoice**:
Made in the app for an order, numbered INV-<agent code>-<seq>, VAT 16% shown; PDF sent on WhatsApp, printable on a Bluetooth printer; pushed to SAP.
_Source_: WS-5 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Mobile Money**:
Payment mode through Airtel or MTN.
_Source_: WS-5 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Quality Manager**:
Quality team member who resolves customer complaints: investigates, fills Operator and Shift, writes the resolution note, sets the statuses.
_Source_: WS-9 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops

**Feedback**:
Customer opinion recorded by a Sales Agent as a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment for each question in the Owner's fixed question list.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-10 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: field-ops
