# Deans Pond HOA

## Language

**HOA**:
The Homeowners Association in the USA whose Board Members and Members use the solution.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Board Member**:
A HOA role with administrative access: manages announcements, tickets, payments, the vendor directory and member information.
_Avoid_: Admin
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Member**:
A HOA role with limited access, based on their own account. The BRD also calls this role "Society Member".
_Avoid_: Society Member
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Member Directory**:
The directory of HOA members.
_Avoid_: HOA Directory
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**General Announcement**:
Information the Board publishes for Members (not event or party related).
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Event / Party Announcement**:
A separate announcement form the Board uses to publish party or event-related information.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Ticket**:
A Member's report of an HOA-related concern or request, handled by Board Members.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Raise Payment**:
A payment request the Board raises for a list of HOA Members; each raise creates one Payment per listed Member.
_Avoid_: Payment request, Payment Collection
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Payment**:
One Member's payment entry, created from a Raise Payment. Tracked only; Members do not pay online.
_Source_: RFP-1, WS-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Payment Reminder**:
A reminder a Member receives for an upcoming payment, approximately one month before the due date.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Overdue Reminder**:
A weekly reminder a Member receives after the due date until the Payment is Paid, Waived or Cancelled.
_Source_: WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Proof of payment**:
A document a Board Member uploads when recording a Payment as Paid; seen by Board Members and the entry's own Member only.
_Source_: WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Invoice**:
A PDF a Member downloads for one Payment: HOA name, Member, title, amount, due date, status; once Paid it adds paid date and method.
_Source_: WS-3 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Vendor**:
A service provider used or recommended by the HOA, listed in the vendor directory (grass cutting / landscaping, plumber, electrician, maintenance and other HOA service providers).
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt

**Government ID Proof**:
A government identity document collected for each vendor; contains sensitive personal information.
_Source_: RFP-1 · _Status_: proposed · _Used in_: hoa-mgmt
