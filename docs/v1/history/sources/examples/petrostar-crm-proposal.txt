Approvals come first. A product only reaches a tender once each operator (PDO, OQ, Oxy, BP, Shell and others)
has approved it, and each operator runs its own process.
Two order types. Own-account orders run through your books. Commission orders go buyer to principal, and
PetroStar earns a pre-agreed commission of around 3%.
Documents decide delivery. ITP, GAD, MTC, WPS/PQR and inspection records must close before goods move.
Margins are sensitive. Cost, margin, commission and principal pricing must stay with a named few.
SAP stays the system of record for orders and finance. The CRM runs alongside it, with no live integration needed
now.
