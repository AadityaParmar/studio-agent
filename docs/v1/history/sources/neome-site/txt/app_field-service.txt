Three gaps account for most of what a field service desk loses, and none of them is a technical skill problem.
Unprioritised
Every request is urgent to the person who raised it
Without a priority and a deadline, the loudest customer is served first rather than the one owed a response.
Unequipped
The engineer arrives without the history
Nobody told them the machine failed twice already, so the visit repeats a diagnosis that was already done.
Unclosed
The job is finished, the paperwork is not
Reports are written that evening from memory, so the record is thinner than the work and billing waits.
Neome closes all three by giving the ticket a deadline, the engineer the history, and the job a signature.
