Projects rarely fail late. They are just found out late.
Three gaps account for most of what goes wrong on a delivery, and none of them is a capability problem.
Unmeasured
Progress is a percentage somebody feels
Without milestones, a project is 80 percent done for two months and nobody can say what is left.
Unlogged
Nobody knows what it actually took
Hours are never recorded, so the next estimate is the last guess with a bit added for comfort.
Unwatched
The overrun arrives fully formed
Spend is compared to budget at the end, by which point the project is the thing that has to absorb it.
Neome closes all three by measuring against milestones, logging the hours, and raising the overrun early.
