A visit nobody can evidence is a visit nobody can improve.
Three gaps account for most of what a field team loses, and none of them is an effort problem.
Unplanned
The day is decided in the car
Without a plan by territory, reps visit who is nearest or friendliest rather than who is due.
Unconfirmed
The visit is reported, not evidenced
A tick in a sheet that evening is a claim, and the manager has no way to tell it from a recollection.
Unrecorded
What came out of the visit stays with the rep
The outcome lives in a notebook, so the next conversation starts from nothing and the follow-up never lands.
Neome closes all three by planning the week, evidencing the call, and capturing the outcome where it happened.
Planning
A week planned by territory, not by proximity
Visits are scheduled against customers and territories, built into daily and weekly plans and sequenced by priority. The plan detects a calendar clash while it is being made, so a rep is never expected in two places at once and discovers it on the day.
