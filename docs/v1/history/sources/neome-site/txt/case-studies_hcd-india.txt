The challenge
HCD manages a large ecosystem of riders, vehicles, branches, rentals, payments, maintenance, complaints, and spare parts. As operations grew, running these activities manually and across disconnected processes became increasingly difficult.
Manual rider onboarding with no centralized view of the rider pipeline.
Vehicle assignment and availability tracked by hand across multiple branches.
Daily rental calculation and collection handled as a manual process.
Rider payments, outstanding balances, and wallet recharges tracked manually.
Limited visibility into vehicle kilometres and actual fleet usage.
Complaint registration and follow-up managed manually, with job work and invoices tracked separately.
Spare-parts and vehicle inventory managed outside any connected system.
No way to connect payment status with vehicle operations.
The Neome solution
Neome designed and built an integrated Rider & Fleet Management Platform covering admin web, rider mobile app, and IoT, so information flows automatically from one stage of the operation to the next instead of each process being digitized in isolation.
Rider lifecycle management
Rider pipeline, onboarding, documents, profile, status, discounts, and history run in one place, from first contact through verification to active rider.
Fleet and branch management
Vehicle master, rider-to-vehicle mapping, availability, movement, and maintenance are tracked branch-wise across every location.
Digital wallet and online payments
Riders recharge online while the platform records transactions, automatic deductions, and outstanding balances in a single ledger.
Automated rental workflow
Daily rental is calculated and deducted automatically, with rider-wise, pending, and historical rental tracking.
Complaint, job work, and invoicing
Rider complaints are assigned, converted into job work with parts and labour drawn from spare-parts inventory, invoiced, and closed with status tracking throughout.
IoT tracking with payment-linked vehicle control
Connected vehicles report location, kilometres, status, and usage. When a rider crosses a configured payment threshold, the supported IoT infrastructure can restrict the vehicle and re-enable it once payment or recharge completes.

Key learnings
Digitizing the whole workflow creates more value than digitizing individual tasks.
Real-time IoT data on kilometres and usage gives management a truer picture of fleet activity.
Automated rental deductions and wallet transactions remove repetitive manual work.
Giving riders direct access to payments, recharge, and complaints reduces dependency on branch teams.
Connecting payment status to vehicle operations creates control while reducing manual intervention.
HCD now runs riders, vehicles, branches, rentals, wallet, payments, complaints, job work, inventory, and IoT as one centralized solution: a more automated, transparent, scalable, and data-driven fleet operation.
