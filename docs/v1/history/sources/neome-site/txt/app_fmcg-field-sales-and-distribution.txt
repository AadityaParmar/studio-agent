The field is not the problem. Not knowing what happened there is.
Three gaps account for most of what a growing field operation loses, and none of them is a selling problem.
Unverified
You are told the beat was worked
Attendance and visits come back as a phone call or a message, so the day is a recollection rather than a record.
Unreconciled
Stock leaves the warehouse and disappears
What an executive carries, sells and returns is squared up at month end, long after anything can be done about it.
Unconnected
Orders, collections and complaints live apart
Each sits in a different book or chat, so nobody can see what an outlet ordered, paid and complained about together.
Neome closes all three by making one app record the day as it happens, with or without a network.

Orders and collections
Traditional retail and modern trade, on their own workflows
A beat order at a kirana counter and a modern trade order against a purchase order are not the same transaction, so they do not share a form. Both draw on one catalog and one price list, and both route to the distributor or depot that will actually fulfil them. A payment taken against either posts straight to that customer's ledger, so the outstanding balance is live and the executive standing in the shop can see what is owed before writing the next order.
