The challenge
Disconnected data, inconsistent reporting, and poor field visibility made daily execution hard to control.
WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, and unstructured phone calls created operational chaos.
Manual order processing took 15-20 minutes and often introduced errors.
Attendance compliance was around 40%, with 30-40 missed logs monthly.
Beat adherence and field activity visibility were not available in real time.
Inventory mismatches appeared across distribution layers.
Teams spent around 2 hours every day on manual follow-ups.
The Neome solution
Neome provided a WhatsApp-like interface with structured field workflows, GPS verification, role-specific forms, and real-time dashboards.
Smart attendance
Time-bound prompts with automatic lockouts and GPS verification for accurate attendance.
Beat planning
Daily route assignment and real-time tracking for field team adherence.
Visit logging
GPS-tagged visits with role-specific forms and photo capture.
Order management
Stakeholder-specific workflows for CNF, super stockist, distributor, and retailer orders.
Inventory control
Live visibility and automated stock adjustments across distribution levels.
Real-time dashboard
Admin visibility across field operations, reporting, and performance.
